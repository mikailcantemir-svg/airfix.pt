Frequently asked questions

Short answers, with the detail on each topic page.

How much does it cost to install air conditioning?

The price depends on the distance between units, the finishes and the capacity. The quote is prepared after we see the space.

How often should maintenance be done?

How often it is needed depends on use, dust, pets and whether the space is a home or a business.

Air conditioner not cooling?

There are several possible causes. Diagnosis confirms what is happening before any work.

Is cleaning the filter enough?

Cleaning the filter helps, but dust on the coils and fan needs a deep wash.

Why ask a technician to install or repair?

Installation and work on the refrigerant circuit should be done by someone qualified to do it.
