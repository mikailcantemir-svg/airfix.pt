Common symptoms that call for a diagnosis

These situations do not identify the fault by themselves, but they are frequent reasons to request a technical check:

  • Air conditioner not cooling — dirty filters, refrigerant issues, electrical faults or other technical causes
  • Unusual noises — fans, vibration or worn parts
  • Water leakage — blocked drainage, incorrect inclination or condensate problems
  • Unit not starting — electrical or control issues that need checking on site
  • Weak airflow — obstruction, dirty filters or reduced performance
  • Performance problems — delayed maintenance or an outdoor unit that cannot work freely

How AirFix approaches air conditioning repair

We focus on identifying the real cause before recommending a repair. Similar symptoms can come from different issues, so a proper diagnosis helps avoid unnecessary work and reduces the chance of the problem returning.

After the assessment, we explain what we found and the recommended next step — repair, cleaning or preventive maintenance when that is the more useful option.
